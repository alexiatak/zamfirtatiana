About

Tatiana Zamfir is a specialist cardiologist at Metropolitan Hospital, and also sees patients at the HealthSpot diagnostic centre in Glyfada. Since 2023 she has also seen patients once a month at HealthSpot Santorini.

She trained in cardiology at Asklepieio Voulas General Hospital. She worked for five years at the Metaxa Cancer Hospital of Piraeus, trained in newer cardiac ultrasound techniques at Hippokrateio General Hospital of Athens, and was then an attending cardiologist and head of the cardio-oncology clinic at Asklepieio Voulas.

She speaks Greek, Romanian, Russian and English.
